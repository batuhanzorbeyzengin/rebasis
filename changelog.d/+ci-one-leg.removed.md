CI runs one test leg — Ubuntu on the declared floor — instead of three. The two it lost were a second Python version, which across this repository's runs caught nothing the floor did not, and a macOS runner, which could not finish: `faiss-cpu` and `torch` each link their own OpenMP runtime there and a process holding both aborts before either library does any work ([faiss-wheels#40](https://github.com/kyamagu/faiss-wheels/issues/40), [pytorch#149201](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/issues/149201)). The decision was about wall clock rather than compute: a hung macOS job held every merge behind it for half an hour at a time. macOS remains the maintainer's own platform and the suite runs there; `rebasis doctor` reports the conflicting pair to anyone who has both installed. Every job now carries a `timeout-minutes`, so a hang costs minutes rather than the six hours a runner will otherwise allow.
